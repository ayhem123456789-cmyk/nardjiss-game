THE EIGHTEENTH YEAR · UNDER THE WINTER MOON

NARDJISS

Queen of the Kingdom of Books
❧ ♕ ❧

To Her Majesty, Queen Nardjiss,

Keeper of the Ancient Library, Lady of Midnight, Reader of Forbidden Stories, and Sovereign of her eighteenth year.

My Queen,

Forgive the hour at which this letter arrives. The moon was already high when I crossed the forest, and the ravens insisted that I turn back.

Naturally, I ignored them.

There are certain occasions upon which even witches must abandon their gloomy towers and ancient spells. Your eighteenth birthday is one of them.

For tonight is not merely the passing of another year. Tonight marks the opening of a new chapter.

THE QUEEN'S

XVIII

TH CHAPTER
يا نرجس، يا ملكة الحكايات والكتب،
في عامكِ الثامن عشر،
أتمنى أن تفتح لكِ الحياة أبواباً
أجمل من كل ما قرأتِه يوماً.
“Some girls inherit kingdoms.
Some girls build their own.”

I have watched kingdoms rise and fall. I have seen princes lose their crowns, emperors lose their empires, and heroes become forgotten names in dusty books.

But I have always believed that the most dangerous person in any kingdom is the girl who knows how to think.

And you, dear Queen, possess a mind that would make even the oldest witch nervous.

So keep your curiosity.

Keep your books.

Keep your strange thoughts that arrive at three in the morning.

Keep the parts of yourself that the world does not immediately understand.

They may be the most precious parts.

♕

As for your eighteenth year...

I have cast a small spell for you.

May you encounter books that become dear friends. May you discover places that feel like scenes from forgotten novels.

May Baghdad's ancient stories whisper something beautiful to you whenever you need it.

May your days contain enough chaos to make them interesting, but enough peace to make them worth living.

And when life becomes another complicated chapter, remember:

✦ You are the author too. ✦
❦ ✦ ❦

There is one final thing I must tell you, Your Majesty.

I could have sent you gold. I could have sent roses. I could have summoned a thousand ravens to sing outside your window.

But instead, I chose words.

Because words are the only magic that survives the person who casts them.

So let these words remain somewhere in your eighteenth chapter:

عيد ميلاد سعيد يا نرجس ♡
وكل عام وأنتِ بخير،
وكل عام وحكايتكِ تصبح أجمل.
May your eighteenth year
be a beautiful curse.

✦ And may the story ahead
be better than the story behind. ✦
